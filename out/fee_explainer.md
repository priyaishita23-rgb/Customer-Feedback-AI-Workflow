## Hidden / unexplained charges on sell orders: what gets deducted when you sell

- For equity delivery, a DP (depository participant) charge applies per sell transaction: Rs 16.5 from Groww plus a depository fee of Rs 3.5 (Rs 3.25 for female account holders).
- DP charges on intraday trades are Rs 0.
- The Groww part of the DP charge is Rs 0 when the debit (sell) value is under Rs 100.
- Opting in to DDPI/POA (the authorisation used to sell holdings) carries a one-time charge of Rs 100 plus GST.
- Statutory charges on equity delivery include STT of 0.1% on buy and sell, stamp duty of 0.015% on buy, exchange transaction charges of 0.00297% (NSE) or 0.00375% (BSE), and SEBI turnover charges of 0.0001%.
- Example (calculated from the listed rates, not a quote): a delivery sell incurs a DP charge of (Rs 16.5 + Rs 3.5) + 18% GST = Rs 23.60 per sell transaction, in addition to brokerage and statutory charges.
