[S1] Groww charges brokerage on equity delivery and intraday orders of Rs 20 or 0.1% of the order value per executed order, whichever is lower, with a minimum of Rs 5.
[S1] For equity delivery, a DP (depository participant) charge applies per sell transaction: Rs 16.5 from Groww plus a depository fee of Rs 3.5 (Rs 3.25 for female account holders).
[S1] The Groww part of the DP charge is Rs 0 when the debit (sell) value is under Rs 100.
[S1] DP charges on intraday trades are Rs 0.
[S1] GST of 18% is applied on brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, IPFT charges, SEBI turnover charges and delayed payment charges.
[S1] Statutory charges on equity delivery include STT of 0.1% on buy and sell, stamp duty of 0.015% on buy, exchange transaction charges of 0.00297% (NSE) or 0.00375% (BSE), and SEBI turnover charges of 0.0001%.
[S1] Opting in to DDPI/POA (the authorisation used to sell holdings) carries a one-time charge of Rs 100 plus GST.
[S1] Auto square-off of an intraday position carries a charge of Rs 50 per position.
[S2] The charges shown in a Groww contract/charges report are grouped as Groww charges (brokerage and DP), external charges (stamp duty, exchange transaction, SEBI turnover, IPFT, depository fee) and taxes (STT and GST).
[S2] For delivery sells, stamp duty is Rs 0; stamp duty applies only on the buy side.
[S2] On intraday trades, STT is Rs 0 on the buy side and 0.025% on the sell side.
[S3] GST is not applied on STT or stamp duty.
[S3] Under SEBI guidelines, brokerage on equity delivery trades cannot exceed 2.5% of the trade value.
[S4] CDSL levies a flat Rs 3.50 per debit transaction on depository participants (tariff effective 1 October 2024), which is the depository fee passed through in the DP charge.
